About Mpho

Research, strategy and accountability for gender justice

Dr Mpho Elizabeth Mpofu has spent over twelve years working across Africa and Europe on gender equality, diversity and inclusion, feminist accountability and violence against women and girls. She holds a PhD in Sociology from the University of Botswana and an MSc in International Development (Gender) from the University of Bristol.

Her work is grounded in a commitment to equity, dignity and the meaningful participation of women, girls and marginalised communities in shaping development and social change.
